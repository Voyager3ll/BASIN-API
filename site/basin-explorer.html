API endpoints behind this page

GET /datasetsLoaded SWAT runs and their outlet loads
GET /practicesPractice catalog: efficiencies, eligible land uses, cost
GET /datasets/{name}/baseline/{level}Baseline loads by hru, subbasin, county or basin
GET /datasets/{name}/outlet/timeseriesMonthly or yearly outlet reach series
POST /datasets/{name}/scenariosRun a scenario like the one on this page
GET /hypoxia/paramsDefault hypoxia model settings
This page runs the same scenario math as the BASIN API prototype, in your browser, on its synthetic 12-subbasin SWAT2012 sample (60 HRUs, 6 sample counties). Practice efficiencies are rounded Iowa Nutrient Reduction Strategy figures, and costs and hypoxia settings are placeholders. None of these numbers describe the real Upper Mississippi River Basin yet.
